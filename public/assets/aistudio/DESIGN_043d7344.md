## Brand & Style

This design system channels the calculated excellence of elite German motorsport paddocks and Japanese tuning houses, tailored for a premier automotive atelier in Sialkot Cantonment. The design ethos communicates uncompromising structural precision, aerospace-grade engineering, and mechanical mastery.

The visual direction merges **Technical Minimalism** with **Refined Dark Glassmorphism**:
- **Atmosphere:** Deep obsidian and cold charcoal chassis layers creating an immaculately organized, cleanroom workshop setting.
- **Precision Detailing:** 1px engineered hairline borders, calibrated status badges, monospaced metadata callouts, and surgical crimson accents that command attention without unnecessary decorative noise.
- **Emotional Resonance:** Authority, technical superiority, absolute reliability, and motorsport heritage.

## Colors

The palette is engineered around high optical contrast and functional clarity in low-light environments.

- **Primary (`#E51A2C`):** Precision Carmine Red. Reserved strictly for primary callouts, vital system alerts, active telemetry, critical workshop booking actions, and high-performance branding indicators.
- **Secondary (`#8E95A5`):** Industrial Chrome Slate. Used for secondary readouts, technical units of measurement, component borders, and subordinate iconography.
- **Tertiary (`#1A1D24`):** Machined Charcoal Surface. Serves as interactive card backgrounds, elevated containers, and segmented tool arrays.
- **Neutral (`#090A0D`):** Deep Chassis Black. The baseline canvas surface, creating maximum dynamic range against crisp stark whites and hot reds.
- **Surface Elevation Levels:**
  - Base Floor: `#090A0D`
  - Mid Structural Bay: `#12141A`
  - Top Worksurface: `#1A1D24`
  - High-Stress / Highlight Border: `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.18)`

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy balances mechanical authority with instantaneous readability under workshop conditions:

- **Headlines (Space Grotesk):** Provides aerodynamic geometry and industrial authority. Uppercase treatment is applied to primary service categories, telemetry labels, and section flags.
- **Body & Longform (Inter):** High-efficiency neutral clarity ensuring diagnostic logs, service breakdowns, and warranty descriptions remain razor sharp at every scale.
- **Technical & Microcopy (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for VIN validation codes, torque values, dyno telemetry, part serial numbers, time stamps, and pricing tables. Always rendered with slight positive tracking for legibility.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a strict **12-column fluid grid** for desktop and tablet architectures, collapsing to a single-column structured pipeline on mobile devices.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - Mobile: `< 640px` (4 columns, `gutter-mobile`, `margin-mobile`)
  - Tablet: `640px - 1024px` (8 columns, `gutter`, `margin-tablet`)
  - Desktop / Workshop Console: `> 1024px` (12 columns, max-width `1440px`, centered, `margin`)
- **Rhythm:** Spacing follows mathematical intervals of 4px/8px. Structural components (diagnostics cards, booking flows) utilize high inner density (`space-sm` to `space-md`), encased by deliberate, breathing perimeter margins (`space-xl`) to preserve focus.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy does not use soft, fuzzy drop shadows; instead, it relies on **structural layered luminosity and precision hairline contours**:

1. **Level 0 (Floor Canvas):** Solid `#090A0D` with an optional microscopic 24px CSS radial dot matrix (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.03)`).
2. **Level 1 (Sub-Assembly / Panels):** `#12141A` with a 1px solid border of `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.12)`.
3. **Level 2 (Active Cards & Tooling Trays):** Semi-transparent `#1A1D24` at 85% opacity with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and a directional 1px perimeter highlight (`rgba(229, 26, 44, 0.25)` top-edge bias or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` surrounding).
4. **Level 3 (Diagnostic Overlays & Modals):** `#161920` with a sharp crimson glow (`0 0 24px rgba(229, 26, 44, 0.15)`) coupled with a solid 1px hairline border (`rgba(229, 26, 44, 0.4)`).

## Shapes

The form language is disciplined, compact, and engineered. Soft, circular treatments are discarded in favor of sharp, technical bevels and subtle radiuses (`0.25rem` standard).

- **Standard Containers & Buttons:** `0.25rem` (4px). Reflects precision-machined billet aluminum parts.
- **Badges, Tags, and Telemetry Pills:** Flat or slightly beveled edge (`0.125rem` / 2px) to resemble asset tags and stamped chassis plates.
- **Interactive Modals & Large Banners:** Maximum `0.5rem` (8px). Corners never feel bulbous or playful; they mimic calibrated electronic equipment and telemetry monitors.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Emergency/Book Service/Confirm Diagnostics):** Background `#E51A2C`, text `#FFFFFF`, font `JetBrains Mono` (Uppercase, `label-lg`). Border: 1px solid `#FF3B4E`. Subtle hover state shifts background to `#C91424` with an inner mechanical glow (`inset 0 0 8px rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary Action (Configure/Telemetry):** Surface `#1A1D24`, text `#FFFFFF`, border 1px solid `rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.3)`. Hover: border brightens to `#8E95A5`.

### Chips & Technical Badges
- Displayed with a monospaced prefix (e.g., `[STAGE-1]`, `[ECU-CALIBRATED]`, `[BAY-04]`).
- Dark background (`#12141A`), 1px perimeter border, left-aligned dot indicator (green for verified, carmine red for maintenance required, amber for scheduled).

### Rating Stars & Feedback Metrics
- High-contrast geometric 5-point stars filled with pure metallic amber (`#FFB800`) or crisp crimson (`#E51A2C`), bordered with 1px hairline slate outlines. Metric readouts (e.g., `4.98 / 5.00`) are rendered in `JetBrains Mono`.

### Cards & Service Enclosures
- Diagnostic cards feature a subtle mechanical top bar (2px crimson indicator for active bay/service).
- Content separated by 1px sub-dividers (`rgba(142, 149, 165, 0.1)`). Background uses high-opacity dark frosted glass (`#12141A` with 8px blur).

### Input Fields & Controls
- Input bays sit recessed on `#090A0D` with a 1px border of `#1A1D24`. 
- Active state: crisp `#E51A2C` outline with zero fuzzy outer spread. Placeholder text uses muted slate (`#8E95A5`).
- Checkboxes and toggles are angular, squared-off switches with metallic red active fills.

### Specialized Component: Dyno / Service Telemetry Gauge
- Segmented LED-style progress tracks with segmented dashes rather than continuous smooth bars, highlighting mechanical precision and exact technical calibration.
